<h1>🌱 Planto</h1>
<h3>
An iOS app that helps people accomplish their daily goals 
by watering and nurturing virtual plants as a reminder mechanic.
</h3>
<p>
Turning daily habits into something worth growing.
</p>
</div>

<h1>About The Project</h1>
<p>
Planto gamifies the process of staying on top of daily reminders 
and goals. Instead of a standard notification or checkbox, users 
nurture a virtual plant — watering it when they complete a task 
and watching it grow as they stay consistent.
</p>
<p>
The app makes routine tasks feel rewarding and personal by 
connecting progress to something visual and alive.
</p>

<h1>Features</h1>
<ul>
<li>Add plants with a custom name, type, and reminder time</li>
<li>Edit and manage existing plants</li>
<li>Progress view showing completed reminders per plant</li>
<li>Full dark mode support</li>
<li>Clean and minimal iOS interface</li>
</ul>
